about Overton

Overton is a prototype of an AI-powered self-reflection companion. It helps structure personal reflection through questions and journaling. It is not a therapist, doctor, or diagnostic tool. In this demonstration, responses are generated by predefined conversational logic and are not sent to an external AI service.

your data

Your journal entries and mood check-ins are stored locally in this browser using local storage. They are not uploaded to a cloud account.
